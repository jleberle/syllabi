This course surveys the United States from the end of the Civil War to the present: Reconstruction and its collapse, industrialization and the labor conflicts it produced, overseas empire, two world wars, the New Deal and the Cold War state, the Black freedom struggle, and the political and economic realignments of the last half-century. The course is built around a single premise: history is an argument about evidence, not a list of things that happened. You will spend most of your graded time in this room, writing by hand, making arguments from lecture material and primary sources.
