## Exam Proctoring

Exams will be proctored using OSU's approved virtual proctoring program, ProctorU/Meazure Learning, with the Record+ setup, which costs roughly $7 per exam and is billed to your bursar account. Information on setting up the program is available on the modules tab of Canvas, and you must complete the ProctorU system check by 11:59PM Central Time on Sunday of week one, so that setup problems surface before Exam 1.

Students who do not wish to use virtual proctoring must email me ahead of time to discuss alternative arrangements. Exams not taken with virtual proctoring will need to be completed by hand in my office or at the testing center, which charges a fee unless it is an accessibility exam. Alternative arrangements **must be approved by me ahead of time**.

## Dropped Scores

Your three lowest lecture video scores, four lowest InQuizitive scores, and lowest exam score are dropped at the end of the semester. This is automatic; you do not have to ask.

## Bonus Points

One standing bonus point opportunity is offered: 25 points for completing every assignment that counts toward your grade. Completing every assignment means the syllabus quiz, at least 36 lecture videos, at least [counted] InQuizitives, and at least three exams, with no zeros on the scores that count. Your dropped scores do not affect the bonus. No other bonus points or extra credit will be offered, and work may not be submitted or revised for additional points.

## Extensions, Makeups, and Late Work

**Deadlines**: All submissions are final once the due date has passed. It is your responsibility to verify the correct assignment is submitted to Canvas and accessible for grading.

**Known absences**: If you know you will be without internet during a period of the semester, contact me ahead of time to arrange early assignment completion.

**Extenuating circumstances**: Extensions are available for extenuating circumstances (unforeseen illness, etc.). You need to alert me as soon as possible and provide relevant dated documentation of the issue in order to be eligible for an extension.

**Technical issues**: Technical issues (e.g. internet outages, technical errors) do not automatically qualify for extensions, so do not wait until the last minute to submit assignments. Each student is afforded one documentation-free extension for a technical issue during the semester; to use it, contact me within 24 hours of the deadline. Subsequent requests due to technical issues will need verifiable documentation of the circumstances to be eligible for an extension.

**Exam makeups**: ProctorU has a bug that prevents makeups through the virtual proctoring system. I have reported it repeatedly, and ProctorU has acknowledged it but not fixed it. ProctorU is the only virtual proctoring the university provides, so the only option for a makeup is on campus, in person in my office or through the testing center (an additional charge applies at the testing center). Plan to take each exam during the week it is due.

**Resubmissions**: Once a grade is posted, the assignment cannot be revised or resubmitted.

# Individual assignments

### Syllabus Quiz

A ten question, pass/fail quiz covering the major parts of the syllabus and class requirements, due at the end of week one.

### Lecture Videos

Lecture videos are posted on the main page of the course: three each week (in week one, an introduction video and two lectures) and one on exam weeks, 39 in all. Each video watched in full earns full credit, each watched at least 50% but not in full earns half credit, and each watched less than 50% earns no credit. Credit comes from Canvas Studio's viewership data, which is the only record used. Videos must be watched the week they are assigned, by 11:59PM Central Time on Sunday, to earn points.

### InQuizitives

InQuizitives are assignments provided by Norton, tied to the textbook chapters on the schedule. They are not a traditional quiz: wrong answers do not automatically cost you, and the activity continues until you reach the point threshold for that assignment. Once you hit the threshold you can keep working for additional practice if you choose to. **InQuizitives are due by 11:59PM Central Time on Thursday of the week they are assigned.** Your four lowest scores are dropped.

### Exams

There will be four exams, each closed note and closed book, taken through ProctorU (see Exam Proctoring). Each exam is 90 minutes long, is worth 200 points, and has three sections, in this order:

- **Essay (100 points)**: Write a thesis-based essay responding to a prompt built from the lectures, supported by specific evidence from the course.
- **Source analysis (60 points)**: Analyze a primary source you have not seen before, using material from both the textbook and the lectures.
- **IDs (40 points)**: Six terms are provided, drawn from the key terms list in each textbook chapter, so you can build your own study list from those chapters. Choose four and identify each one and its significance.

**Each section locks once you complete it, and you cannot return to an earlier section.** Proofread and edit before moving on: treat each section as one you turn in before receiving the next. Plan your time by the points each section is worth: about 45 minutes for the essay, 25 for the source analysis, and 15 for the IDs, which leaves 5 minutes to spare.

Each exam is available in ProctorU from 9AM Central Time on Monday of the week it is due until 9:45PM Central Time on its due date (Sunday, or Wednesday for Exam 4). **The last time to start an exam is 9:45PM.** ProctorU does not allow a partial exam, so you cannot start one you could not finish by the deadline, and the 9:45PM cutoff also leaves room for check-in delays. Your lowest exam score is dropped.

# Course Policies

## Weekly Schedule

The course runs on a Monday-Sunday schedule, and each Monday by 9AM the week's module opens and the course front page is updated with that week's work. All deadlines are 11:59PM Central Time:

- **Thursday**: that week's InQuizitives
- **Sunday**: that week's lecture videos, and any exam or other assignment due that week (exams must be **started by 9:45PM**; see Exams)

Finals week has no assigned course materials, and Exam 4 is due on Wednesday of finals week and must be **started by 9:45PM**.

You must have access to reliable internet throughout the semester. If you miss a full week of work, I will reach out to check in. If something is getting in the way, tell me early: extensions depend on prompt notice.
