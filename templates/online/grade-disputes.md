Students wishing to dispute their grades (outside of obvious mathematical errors or clarification of comments) are required to wait 24 hours after the assignment has been handed back and then, within two weeks of its return, need to attend student hours (or schedule a meeting) to formally discuss the grade they received. Be prepared to come to the meeting with specific points you feel were not taken into account with your grade. I reserve the right to raise *or* lower your grade at these meetings.

Due to the Federal Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), I **do not** discuss grades over email or through Canvas comments on assignments. Students who wish to discuss a grade, or who are unsure where they stand in the class, need to see me in student hours or through an appointment, over Zoom or in person.
