Students wishing to dispute their grades (outside of obvious mathematical errors or clarification of comments) are required to wait 24 hours after the assignment has been handed back and then, within two weeks of its return, need to attend student hours (or schedule a meeting, in person or by Zoom outside student hours) **with the person who graded their work** to formally discuss the grade they received. Please come to the meeting with specific points you feel were not taken into account by your grader. If I did not grade your assignment I will only meet with you *after* you have talked to the TA. We reserve the right to raise *or* lower your grade at these meetings. If informal discussions do not resolve the issue, final-grade appeals follow OSU Policy and Procedures 2-0821 (Appeal of Final Grade), available through the Office of Academic Affairs (101 Whitehurst, 405-744-5627).

Under the Federal Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), we must verify who we are speaking with before discussing grades, so we **cannot** discuss grades over email. If you are unsure where you stand in the class, please see us in student hours, or schedule a Zoom meeting outside student hours.
