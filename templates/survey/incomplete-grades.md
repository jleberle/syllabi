University policy requires that students complete *more than 50%* of the course grade, have a grade of D or better, and be unavoidably prevented from finishing the remaining work to receive an "incomplete" grade. While I will only give incompletes in extremely rare situations, be advised you will need to finish the coursework to remove the incomplete, otherwise you will receive the grade you earned without the uncompleted work. **Incompletes are not automatic**, you must discuss an incomplete with me ahead of time and coordinate a plan for completing the course material.
