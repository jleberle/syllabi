These are the services you are most likely to need; the [OSU Syllabus Attachment](https://academicaffairs.okstate.edu/student-support/) lists the rest.

- [LASSO Center](https://lasso.okstate.edu): Free tutoring and academic success coaching
- [OSU Writing Center](https://cas.okstate.edu/osu_writing_center/): Help with brainstorming, drafting, and editing your writing
- [Student Accessibility Services](https://accessibility.okstate.edu/): Register for accommodations (see Accommodations)
- [University Counseling Services](https://ucs.okstate.edu/): Counseling, including emergency support options
- [Care Report](https://reportconcern.okstate.edu): Help with any challenge, including food, housing, and other basic needs
