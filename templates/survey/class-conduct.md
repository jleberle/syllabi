**Arriving and leaving**: Please avoid coming to class late or departing early. Either must have my approval to reduce disruptions. I will have the TAs step outside and restrict entry after 5 minutes, except on exam days, to minimize disruptions. Do not pack up prior to class being formally dismissed. Packing up early is not only disrespectful but causes too much noise for others to properly hear the lecture.

**Devices**: You may use a laptop only to take notes for the course, you will be asked to leave if you are using it for anything besides course notes. Phones may not be used in any circumstance and headphones must be taken off during the class period.

**Recording**: Taking photographs, videos, or audio recordings (including automatic transcription) of the lectures or PowerPoint slides is strictly prohibited, as is the use of smart glasses. Recording is allowed only as an accommodation approved through SAS.

**Respect**: You are expected to conduct yourself in a professional manner. History has a number of controversial events and we will address some of these. Please respect the views of your classmates and treat everyone with decency.

**Strikes**: Students are provided two strikes for violations of class conduct. After the second strike, technology privileges are lost for the semester. Privileges may be restored at my discretion after an in-person meeting with me to discuss the situation.
