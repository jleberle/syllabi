If you are registered with [Student Accessibility Services](https://accessibility.okstate.edu) (SAS), you are required to meet with me, in person or over Zoom, to discuss your accommodations. Your SAS letter does not replace this meeting, and it cannot be held over email. Accommodations take effect once we meet.

**Note especially:** the graded work in this course is overwhelmingly handwritten and timed, and some workshops use audio or video sources. If handwriting, extended time, a reduced-distraction room, captioning, or assistive technology is relevant to you, make sure you have registered with SAS. SAS can also cover temporary issues (e.g. broken wrist) or chronic medical conditions (e.g. diabetes, migraines, etc.)

Workshops are short, and most students work with the same time allotment as everyone else. If you need extended time or another adjustment on a workshop, talk to me in student hours and I will accommodate it as best I can.
